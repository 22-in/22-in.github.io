# Privacy Policy for Arena22

**Last Updated:** June 2026

Thank you for choosing to use Arena22! We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what data our Discord bot collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

## 1. Data We Collect
To provide our services (Voice Channel XP, Server Boss system, and Search functions), Arena22 collects the bare minimum data required:
* **Discord User IDs:** To track points and identify users on the leaderboard.
* **Discord Guild (Server) IDs:** To keep leaderboards and boss settings specific to each server.
* **Voice Channel State:** To check if a user is actively in a voice channel (unmuted/undeafened) to award points. We **DO NOT** record or listen to your voice/audio.
* **Presence Data (Online Status):** To detect when the Server "Boss" comes online and announce their arrival.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
* **Leaderboards:** To calculate and display voice channel activity points.
* **Boss System:** To grant special permissions (`!bossay`) and announce the top user.
* **Functionality:** To ensure commands execute properly within the correct server environment.

## 3. Data Sharing and Security
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, trade, or rent your data to any third parties.
* **Security:** Your data is stored securely in an encrypted database (MongoDB Atlas). 
* **Search Queries:** The `!ask` command fetches data from public search engines. Your queries are not permanently logged or tied to your identity in our database.

## 4. Data Deletion
If you wish to have your data removed:
* **Server Owners:** Kicking the bot from your server stops all future data collection.
* **Users:** If you want your specific User ID wiped from our database, you can contact the developer.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in our support server or via bot updates.

**Contact Us:** If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to the bot developer on Discord.
